Create a premium, sophisticated, single-page website for “London Advanced Aesthetic Clinic” using Next.js.

The website should feel like a real, high-end London aesthetic clinic designed by an experienced human creative director. It must not look AI-generated, template-based, generic, overly futuristic, or like a typical SaaS website.

Before designing, research leading London aesthetic clinics and luxury beauty/medical brands to understand their visual hierarchy, wording, treatment presentation, trust-building, and conversion patterns. Take inspiration from the level of refinement and restraint used by premium London clinics, but do not copy any existing website.

Core Visual Direction
Use a strict Pure Gold + White visual identity.

Colours
Primary background: pure white #FFFFFF
Primary gold: elegant metallic-style gold such as #C9A227
Dark text: very deep charcoal/black #171717
Very light warm neutral may be used sparingly for separation if necessary
Gold should be used as an accent, not as a large background colour
No gradients anywhere
No excessive beige, cream, pink, purple, blue, or artificial luxury colours
The overall feeling should be:

Quiet luxury + London sophistication + medical credibility + feminine refinement + premium restraint.

Do not make the website flashy.

Do not use excessive gold borders.

Do not use gold everywhere.

Gold should appear selectively on buttons, small labels, icons, dividers, borders, and important details.

Typography
Do not use generic AI/default website fonts.

Choose a refined font combination that suits a luxury aesthetic clinic.

Recommended direction:

Elegant editorial serif for major headlines
Clean, sophisticated sans-serif for body text, navigation, buttons, and supporting information
Consider premium-feeling combinations such as:

Cormorant Garamond + Manrope
DM Serif Display + Inter
Playfair Display + Manrope
Instrument Serif + Manrope
Choose the combination that looks the most sophisticated and natural for a London aesthetic clinic.

Typography should feel editorial and expensive without becoming difficult to read.

Use generous spacing and strong typographic hierarchy.
